📜 How to Play

Ahoy, sailor! Follow these steps to escape the Flying Dutchman.

  1. Challenge 1 — The Captain's Quiz: Answer 5 general knowledge questions. You need at least 3 correct answers to pass.
  2. Challenge 2 — The Secret Code: Solve the three clues to discover the secret code.
  3. Challenge 3 — The Hidden Emoji: Enter your name and find the hidden skull before the 60-second timer ends.
  4. Escape! Complete all three challenges to break the curse and win the game.

Good luck, sailor! ☠️
